# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in Astra's issues, pull requests and other project spaces is expected to help maintain a respectful, welcoming environment.
This also applies when representing the project in public.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and observable behavior with clear, constructive feedback.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and communication styles.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and make a reasonable effort to resolve misunderstandings.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, doxxing and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable. Technical
disagreement is welcome; abuse directed at a person is not.

## Reporting and response

Report immediate threats through the appropriate emergency or platform channels. For project conduct concerns, contact a maintainer through an
existing private channel. If none is available, use GitHub's reporting tools or open a minimal issue requesting a private contact channel. Keep
identities, allegations and private evidence out of public requests. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review the available context and may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation
temporarily or permanently. Responses should be proportionate, explain the conduct at issue where possible, and protect private reporting details.
A person affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through the same private channel.

Maintainers are subject to these expectations too. A report involving a maintainer should go to another available maintainer or GitHub's reporting
tools. This project does not promise a dedicated conduct team, private contact address or response-time guarantee.
